About Us

We don't just film — we uncover

Salt & Pepper Productions is a Birmingham based videographer editor duo turning ideas into cinematic visuals. Manasseh captures the story with dynamic, intentional camera work. Thomas brings it to life with sharp, seamless editing. Together, we deliver high quality content for brands, artists, and creators who want visuals that stand out.

From short films, music videos and lyric visualizers to long-form documentary features, our work is defined by patience, presence and a deep respect for the subject in front of our lens.
